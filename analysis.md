### Step 1 - Analyze all film tod etermine plays
Read through all film and determine the start time and end time of each play and what parts of the videos are not plays. With that data we can cut the clips of the videos of each play allowing the user to see whats been cut. 

#### Notes
- Hours of film. First decide whether a stretch is a live play or something else.
- An offensive or defensive play (teams lined up, snap until the ball is dead) is kept.
- If it is not that, check special teams (kickoff, punt, field goal, extra point, or the return). That is kept too.
- Anything else is junk and is discarded.
- Unsure stretches are set aside. After the film is read they are looked at once more. Whatever is still unsure is left for a person to mark as offense/defense, special teams, or junk.
- Deep charting of each play comes after the film is cut.

### Step 2 - Your team
You choose which uniform is yours. The next read is told that. A white jersey that is defending is your defense, and the other team is on offense.

### Step 3 - Chart the play
That read names a scrimmage play as a run or a pass, and a special-teams play as the kick, punt, or return from your side.

### Step 4 - Formation, experimental
Last. For your offensive plays only, name the formation the offense is lined up in. It shows on the play, in the scouting report, and can be turned on in Settings.
