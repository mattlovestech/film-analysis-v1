      How a film becomes plays

      1. DownloadThe full film is saved before it is read.
      2. ReadEach stretch is a play, junk, or not clear yet. On Cloudflare, more workers read at once.
      3. CutA play is clipped only after the film up to that point has settled, so the clips stay in order.
      4. CheckA stretch that is still unclear gets one more look. What remains waits for you to mark it.

      Charting a play comes later, after its clip is final.
