        How a film becomes plays

        1. DownloadThe full film is saved before it is read.
        2. ReadEach stretch is a play, junk, or not clear yet. On Cloudflare, more workers read at once.
        3. CutA play is clipped only after the film up to that point has settled, so the clips stay in order.
        4. CheckA stretch that is still unclear gets one more look. What remains waits for you to mark it.
        5. Scout reportIn report settings, pick the team being scouted. That only swaps who counts as offense and defense. The read names a scrimmage play as a run or a pass from that side. A special-teams play is the kick, punt, or return from that team.
        6. FormationExperimental, and last. After run or pass is known, it plots 11 players on each side and names the formation from that plot.

        Experimental, and last. Off until you turn it on. After run or pass is charted, it plots both teams (always 11, with low-confidence guesses filled in) and names the formation. Turn this on so scouting report charting includes it. On each play you can detect again, override, or clear the guess.

        Charting a play comes later, after its clip is final.
